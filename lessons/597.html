高中高三英语《完形填空技巧》教案

高中高三英语完形填空技巧

📝 备课方案

**教学目标** 1. 理解高考完形填空"语义为主、语法为辅"的命题特点，掌握"整体把握—先易后难—回读验证"的解题流程。 2. 学会利用上下文逻辑线索（因果、转折、并列、递进、例证）判断答案，克服"就空论空、孤立答题"的弊病。 3. 掌握动词、名词、形容词、副词等实词的词义辨析方法，重点突破近义词在语境中的细微差别。 4. 熟悉常见固定搭配、短语动词与逻辑连接词，能结合语篇主旨与情感线索锁定最佳答案。 **教学重点**：上下文逻辑线索的捕捉与利用；实词词义辨析与固定搭配的积累。 **教学难点**：近义词在具体语境中的语义侧重；依托语篇主旨与作者情感基调的全局性判断。 **教学方法**：语篇整体教学法、逻辑线索分析训练、错因归类总结法，突出"先完意、后完形"。 **教学准备**：多媒体课件、近五年高考完形真题汇编、逻辑信号词清单、高频固定搭配表、限时训练学案。 **课时安排**：共 2 课时。第 1 课时命题特点与整体策略（语篇把握、首句原则），第 2 课时逻辑线索与词义辨析专题（真题精讲、错因归类、限时演练），并完成小结。

📖 课堂讲义 · 核心知识点

**一、命题特点与总策略** 完形填空是"阅读＋词汇"综合题型。首句通常不设空，是主题窗口；空格以实词为主；四选项词性相同，干扰项多为形近义近词，答案靠语境判定。三步走：第一步通读全文（跳过空格），把握主线、人物与情感基调；第二步逐空分析，利用前后句与全文主旨选答案，先易后难；第三步回读验证，检查语篇连贯与逻辑通顺。最高原则是"语篇意识"——与主旨、情感基调不符的选项，局部通顺也不能选。 **二、上下文逻辑线索** 五类：转折（but/however/yet，前后相反）；因果（because/so，可互相推出）；并列递进（and/moreover，一致或更深）；让步（although，主句从句相反）；例证（for example，选项是前文观点的例证）。此外，代词指代、同义词复现、反义词对比、上下义词照应都是线索。先圈出空格前后的逻辑信号词，确定空格应取正向或反向。 **三、词义辨析与固定搭配** 动词辨析关注动作发出者、对象与方式（look/watch/see/notice 差别在主动性）；形容词辨析关注情感色彩，要与人物情感匹配；名词辨析关注概念外延与上下文照应。固定搭配与短语动词是高频考点（depend on/turn to/break down），按"动词＋介词"归类积累。连接词也常作选项，看逻辑关系：转折选 however 类，因果选 therefore 类，补充选 besides 类。

🔍 课堂讲义 · 重点难点解析

**重点一：首句原则** 例题：首句"It was the first time Sarah had volunteered at the animal shelter, and she was both nervous and excited." 分析：首句揭示人物、事件、地点与情感基调。 解答：全文空格应围绕"初次志愿服务的紧张与投入"，忌选与基调相反的词。 小结：首句是全文"题眼"。 **重点二：转折与因果线索** 例题：Tom trained hard every day, ______ he still lost the race. 分析：前后相反，填转折词。 解答：but/yet。 小结：转折词后内容与前文相反。 **难点一：近义词辨析** 例题：He ______ at the door before knocking, unsure whether his friend would recognize him. 解答：hesitated（犹豫）最符合"不确定"的心理；waited 仅表等待。 小结：结合心理与情境选词。 **难点二：全局判断** 例题：老人捐积蓄给小学，末段"The old man said his ______ was to see children read and grow." 分析：hope/money/dream 局部都通，须结合"捐资办学"主旨。 解答：选 dream（心愿），统领"希望孩子成才"的主旨。 小结：局部可通时回到全文主旨选最优。

✏️ 课堂讲义 · 典型例题精讲

**例题 1（转折逻辑）** Anna felt lonely in the new town; her classmates seemed cold. (1)____, she decided not to give up. She joined the choir and (2)____ every practice. To her joy, the members welcomed her. (1) A However B Therefore C Besides D Meanwhile (2) A missed B attended C cancelled D avoided 解析：(1) 前后相反，选 However；(2) 由"被欢迎"反推坚持参加，选 attended。答案 A、B。 **例题 2（综合语篇）** Grandpa's garden was his pride. He planted tomatoes and beans and (1)____ watered them daily. One summer, a drought hit. (2)____ his efforts, the plants turned yellow. (1) A carefully B rarely C never D carelessly (2) A Thanks to B In spite of C In addition to D Because of 解析：(1) 以花园为傲且每天浇水，选 carefully；(2) 尽管努力仍枯黄，表让步，选 In spite of。答案 A、B。

📋 分层作业（基础 / 中等 / 难 / 综合）

基础 · 基础巩固

  1. 1. She was very ______ when she heard the good news, jumping up and down with joy. A. sad B. excited C. bored D. tired
    答：B。由"jumping up and down with joy"可推出她听到好消息很兴奋，故选 excited。利用后文动作印证情感。
  2. 2. The meeting was ______ because the manager was ill, and it was put off to next Monday. A. cancelled B. held C. attended D. started
    答：A。由"put off to next Monday"（推迟到周一）可知会议被取消了，故选 cancelled。因果逻辑：经理生病导致取消。
  3. 3. He ______ the books on the desk and found his favorite novel missing. A. went through B. looked down C. gave up D. turned off
    答：A。由"found his favorite novel missing"可知他翻找了桌上的书，"go through"意为"翻查、浏览"，符合语境；look down 为"俯视"，give up 为"放弃"，turn off 为"关闭"，均不符。
  4. 4. Tom was ______ in the race at first, but he tried his best and finally won. A. behind B. ahead C. outside D. beside
    答：A。由"but he tried his best and finally won"可知起初他在比赛中落后，"behind"（落后）与后文"最终获胜"构成转折对比，故选 A。

中等 · 能力提升

  1. 1. When the fire alarm rang, people rushed out of the building ______. Nobody was hurt. A. quickly B. slowly C. lazily D. secretly
    答：A。火灾警报响起，人们应迅速撤离，且"Nobody was hurt"印证撤离及时有效，故选 quickly（迅速地）。结合常识与结果推断。
  2. 2. The teacher ______ the students' answers carefully before giving them feedback, because she wanted to point out every mistake. A. examined B. ignored C. doubted D. avoided
    答：A。由"wanted to point out every mistake"可知老师仔细检查答案，"examined"（仔细检查）符合；ignored（忽视）、avoided（回避）与"指出每个错误"的目的矛盾。因果逻辑：目的是仔细检查。
  3. 3. Although it rained heavily, the football match ______ as planned. The players played in the rain with great passion. A. went on B. broke down C. ran out D. turned up
    答：A。由"Although it rained heavily"与"as planned"可知比赛照常进行，"go on"意为"继续"，符合；break down（出故障）、run out（用完）、turn up（出现）均不符。
  4. 4. Mother was ______ when she saw the mess in the kitchen, but she said nothing and began to clean it up quietly. A. pleased B. annoyed C. excited D. relaxed
    答：B。看到厨房一团糟，母亲应是恼火（annoyed），但"said nothing and began to clean up"又体现她的克制，故选 B；pleased/excited/relaxed 均与"mess"情境矛盾。注意"but"转折后的克制动作反推前文的负面情绪。

难 · 综合挑战

  1. 1. 短文：Mark loved hiking. One day, he got lost in the forest when a sudden storm came. He was ______ at first, but soon calmed down and found a cave to hide in. The next morning, he followed the stream and finally ______ to a village. 第 1 空：A. panicked B. delighted C. satisfied D. relaxed。第 2 空：A. got B. led C. made his way D. turned
    答：第 1 空选 A。迷路遇暴雨，最初应是惊慌（panicked），"but soon calmed down"构成转折，先惊慌后冷静；delighted/satisfied/relaxed 与"突然暴风雨、迷路"的情境矛盾。第 2 空选 C。由"followed the stream"可知他顺着溪流设法到达村庄，"make one's way to"意为"前往、到达"，符合；get 缺宾语结构不符，led 主语应为溪流，turned 语义不全。
  2. 2. 短文：The old house had been empty for years. Grass grew wild in the yard, and the windows were ______. When Sara first saw it, she was about to leave, but the moment she stepped inside, the warm light through the broken roof made the dusty room look almost magical. 题目：A. broken B. clean C. shining D. locked
    答：A。由"Grass grew wild"与后文"broken roof"可知房屋年久失修，窗户应也是破损的，选 broken；clean/shining 与荒废景象矛盾，locked 文中未提及。注意同语境词汇（wild, empty, broken）的语义照应。
  3. 3. 短文：Emma practiced the piano for three hours every day, ______ her fingers ached. Her teacher once said, "Talent may open a door, but ______ keeps it open." 第 1 空：A. so that B. even though C. because D. if。第 2 空：A. luck B. practice C. money D. chance
    答：第 1 空选 B。每天练琴三小时与手指酸痛构成让步关系，"even though"（尽管）符合；so that 表目的、because 表原因、if 表条件，均不符。第 2 空选 B。由"Talent may open a door"与全文"每日苦练"的主旨，可知"练习"才是让门一直开着的关键，选 practice；全文情感基调与主题句共同指向"勤练"。
  4. 4. 短文：The rescue team searched the mountain for two days. Just as hope was fading, they heard a weak ______ from the cave. A. silence B. voice C. picture D. smell
    答：B。由"they heard"可知是从洞穴传来的微弱声音，"voice"（声音、嗓音）符合；silence 为"寂静"、picture 为"图画"、smell 为"气味"，均不能被"heard"接住。动词与宾语的搭配是此题线索。

综合 · 高考真题对接

  1. 1. （2023 年新高考改编）短文：I remember the first time I volunteered at the nursing home. An old woman named Grace sat alone, staring out of the window. I walked over and asked if she would like to join the games. To my surprise, she smiled and ______ me to sit beside her. 题目：A. invited B. warned C. forced D. ordered
    答：A。由"she smiled"可知老人态度友善，邀请（invited）我坐在她身边；warned（警告）、forced（强迫）、ordered（命令）均与微笑的善意矛盾。利用表情线索与人物态度选词。
  2. 2. （2022 年新高考改编）短文：The young painter's works were once considered strange by the public. ______, a famous critic praised them for their honesty and creativity, which changed everything. 题目：A. As a result B. However C. For example D. In addition
    答：B。前句"公众认为奇怪"，后句"著名评论家盛赞"，语义相反，表转折，选 However；As a result 表结果、For example 表例证、In addition 表补充，均不符逻辑。
  3. 3. （2021 年新高考改编）短文：For years, the village had been cut off from the outside world. The building of the new road ______ the lives of the villagers completely, allowing fresh vegetables to reach the city market and children to reach school easily. 题目：A. transformed B. ruined C. ignored D. delayed
    答：A。由后文"allowing fresh vegetables to reach the city market and children to reach school"可知新公路彻底改变了（transformed）村民生活，且是积极改变；ruined（毁坏）与"菜到市场、孩子上学"的积极结果矛盾，ignored/delayed 语义不符。
  4. 4. （2020 年新高考改编）短文：Ben's grandfather taught him to fish when he was six. Years later, when Ben taught his own son, he realized that the greatest gift his grandfather had given him was not the skill itself but the ______ of sitting together in silence by the river. 题目：A. joy B. trouble C. noise D. danger
    答：A。由"not the skill itself but the ______ of sitting together"可知真正的礼物是陪伴的快乐，选 joy；trouble（麻烦）、noise（噪音）、danger（危险）均与"最珍贵的礼物"这一主旨相悖。注意"not...but..."结构对空格语义的提示。

💬 课后反馈

**常见错误** 1 一上来就逐空填空，缺乏整体把握。2 只看到空格附近一两句，忽视全文主旨。3 近义词辨析凭语感，被表面通顺的选项迷惑。4 固定搭配不牢。5 做完不回读验证。 **教学改进建议** 1 强制"通读—填空—回读"三遍法。2 整理"逻辑信号词清单"与"高频搭配表"，每日滚动记忆。3 每次训练给错题标注"错因标签"。 **分层辅导** 学困生先练逻辑线索明显的题目；学优生挑战近义词辨析与主旨统摄类题目。
