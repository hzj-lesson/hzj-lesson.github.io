初中九年级英语《阅读理解技巧（中考）》教案

初中九年级英语阅读理解技巧（中考）

📝 备课方案

**教学目标** 1. 了解中考阅读理解的命题形式与常考题型：细节理解、主旨大意、推理判断、词义猜测、标题归纳。 2. 掌握各类题型的解题策略：细节题定位原文、主旨题抓主题句、推断题依据原文、词义题利用上下文与构词法。 3. 能识别常见干扰项类型（无中生有、偷换概念、以偏概全、绝对化表述），提高排除干扰的能力。 4. 通过限时训练与真题演练，提升阅读速度与答题准确率，培养良好的阅读习惯。 **教学重点**：细节理解题的定位方法；主旨大意题的主题句判断。 **教学难点**：推理判断题的"基于原文、不过度推断"；词义猜测题的多种线索运用；标题归纳题对全文的概括。 **教学方法**：任务驱动法、题型分类训练法、限时训练法、错题分析法。 **教学准备**：多媒体课件、中考阅读真题汇编、阅读策略清单、计时器与限时训练学案。 **课时安排**：共 2 课时。第 1 课时讲解四大题型与解题策略，示范一篇完整阅读；第 2 课时分类专项训练与限时综合训练。

📖 课堂讲义 · 核心知识点

**一、中考阅读五大题型** 1. 细节理解题：就具体信息设问，回原文定位，注意"同义转换"。 2. 主旨大意题：概括文章中心，找主题句——多在首段首句、末段或段首句。 3. 推理判断题：根据文中信息推断隐含内容，答案必须基于原文。 4. 词义猜测题：利用定义解释、同位语、对比转折、构词法、生活常识猜测。 5. 标题归纳题：正确标题要能涵盖全文主题，既不能过大也不能过小。 **二、常见干扰项类型** 1. 无中生有：文中没有的信息。 2. 偷换概念：改动词、数量、时间、地点等关键信息。 3. 以偏概全：用部分细节代替整体主旨。 4. 绝对化表述：出现 all, never, always 等绝对词通常错误。 **三、不同体裁的阅读策略** 记叙文：抓六要素（时间、地点、人物、起因、经过、结果）与情感态度。 说明文：锁定说明对象与主要特征，注意数字、例子、比较。 应用文（广告、通知、表格）：按题干关键词快速查找，不必逐句阅读。

🔍 课堂讲义 · 重点难点解析

**重点一：细节理解题的定位方法** 例题：The museum opens from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on weekdays. 问：When does the museum open on weekdays? A. At 8:00 a.m. B. At 9:00 a.m. C. At 5:00 p.m. D. At 6:00 p.m. 分析：题干关键词 open + weekdays，回原文定位 opens from 9:00 a.m.。 解答：选 B。 小结：读题干圈关键词、回原文找对应句、比对选项排干扰。 **难点一：推理判断题的"度"** 例题：Tom saved his pocket money for six months to buy a gift for his mother. 问：What can we infer from the sentence? A. Tom is poor. B. Tom loves his mother very much. C. Tom has no friends. D. Tom's mother is ill. 分析：攒钱买礼物可推断 Tom 很爱妈妈；其余选项原文无依据。 解答：选 B。 小结：推断题答案必须能从原文推出，且不能过度推断。 **难点二：词义猜测题的线索** 例题：The book is so boring that I fell asleep reading it. boring 意为？ 分析：由"看书时睡着了"推断书很"无聊"，属结果线索。 解答：boring 意为"无聊的"。 小结：优先找解释、例子、转折对比、因果关系等线索。

✏️ 课堂讲义 · 典型例题精讲

**例题 1（细节理解）** 题目：Volunteers helped the players and visitors at the 2022 Winter Olympics. 问：Who helped them? A. The players B. The volunteers C. The visitors D. The teachers 解析：原文直接说明，选 B。 方法小结：答案常是原文直接改写。 **例题 2（主旨大意）** 题目：Reading is important. It opens a new world and helps us learn more. So we should read more. 问：What is the passage mainly about? A. How to read fast B. Why reading is important C. Where to buy books D. Who likes reading 解析：首句是主题句，选 B。 方法小结：主旨题看首尾句。 **例题 3（推理判断）** 题目：Lily was nervous at first, but she took a deep breath and began to sing beautifully. 问：What can we learn? A. She didn't like singing. B. She was afraid of singing at first. C. She was good at dancing. D. She never sang before. 解析：nervous at first 说明起初紧张，选 B；A 与后文矛盾，C/D 无依据。 方法小结：推断须基于原文。

📋 分层作业（基础 / 中等 / 难 / 综合）

基础 · 基础巩固

  1. 1. 阅读：The school library is open from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. from Monday to Friday. 问：The library is open for ______ hours on weekdays. A. 8 B. 9 C. 10 D. 11
    答：答案 C。从上午 8 点到下午 6 点共 10 个小时，为细节计算题。
  2. 2. 阅读：My father usually goes to work by bike, but sometimes he takes the bus. 问：How does my father usually go to work? A. On foot B. By bike C. By bus D. By car
    答：答案 B。usually goes to work by bike，usually 是关键词，细节定位。
  3. 3. 阅读：Tom's favorite subject is English because he thinks it is interesting. 问：Why does Tom like English best? A. Because it is easy B. Because it is interesting C. Because it is useful D. Because it is difficult
    答：答案 B。because he thinks it is interesting，原句直接给出原因。
  4. 4. 阅读：There are 45 students in our class. 25 of them are boys. 问：How many girls are there in the class? A. 15 B. 20 C. 25 D. 45
    答：答案 B。45 减 25 得 20，细节计算题。

中等 · 能力提升

  1. 1. 阅读：Reading is like opening a window to the world. It helps us learn new things and think better. Everyone should read every day. 问：What is the main idea of the passage? A. Reading is a waste of time. B. Reading is good for us. C. Reading is difficult. D. Reading is only for students.
    答：答案 B。主题句为 Reading helps us learn and think better，全篇说明阅读对我们有好处。
  2. 2. 阅读：The little dog is very smart. It can open the door by itself when its owner is not at home. 问：What can we infer from the passage? A. The dog can open the door. B. The dog is lazy. C. The owner never goes out. D. The dog can't stay at home.
    答：答案 A。原文直接说明小狗会自己开门，属于可推断/可得知的信息；B/C/D 无依据。
  3. 3. 阅读：My cousin is very outgoing. She likes making friends and talking with others. 问：The underlined word "outgoing" means ______. A. 内向的 B. 外向的 C. 懒惰的 D. 勤奋的
    答：答案 B。由"喜欢交朋友、爱和别人交谈"推断 outgoing 意为"外向的"。
  4. 4. 阅读：Although the task was difficult, we didn't give up and finally succeeded. 问：What is the best title for the passage? A. Never Give Up B. A Difficult Task C. My Friends D. How to Study
    答：答案 A。全文强调"不放弃、最终成功"，Never Give Up（永不放弃）最能概括主题。

难 · 综合挑战

  1. 1. 阅读：The Great Wall is one of the greatest wonders in the world. It is more than 20,000 kilometers long. Every year, millions of tourists visit it. 问：How long is the Great Wall? A. About 2,000 km B. More than 20,000 km C. Less than 20,000 km D. 200 km
    答：答案 B。It is more than 20,000 kilometers long 直接定位，注意数字细节不被干扰项改动。
  2. 2. 阅读：Nowadays, many students spend too much time on their phones. As a result, they have less time to do exercise and their eyesight gets worse. 问：Which of the following is TRUE according to the passage? A. Students can use phones as much as they like. B. Too much phone use is bad for students' health. C. Phones are useless to students. D. Students should never use phones.
    答：答案 B。原文说玩手机导致锻炼时间减少、视力变差，可推断过多使用手机有害健康；A 相反，C/D 绝对化且无依据。
  3. 3. 阅读：When the sun goes down, the temperature drops quickly in the desert. So travelers must bring warm clothes even in summer. 问：What does the word "drops" mean in the passage? A. 上升 B. 下降 C. 停止 D. 保持不变
    答：答案 B。由"旅行者即使在夏天也要带厚衣服"推断温度"下降"很快，drops 意为下降。
  4. 4. 阅读：Teamwork is very important in a basketball game. Each player should pass the ball to teammates and follow the coach's plan. Only in this way can the team win. 问：What can we infer from the passage? A. One player can win the game alone. B. Players should work together to win. C. The coach never watches the game. D. Basketball is an easy sport.
    答：答案 B。全篇强调团队合作，Each player should pass...Only in this way can the team win，推断获胜需要合作；A/C/D 均无依据。

综合 · 中考真题对接

  1. 1. （中考真题）阅读：Waste sorting is becoming popular in China. People put different kinds of rubbish into different bins. 问：Why do people sort waste? A. To make money. B. To protect the environment. C. To save time. D. To make the bins beautiful.
    答：答案 B。垃圾分类的目的通常与环保相关，原文语境支持"保护环境"；A/C/D 无原文依据。
  2. 2. （中考真题）阅读：The earlier you get up, the more time you have to prepare for the day. 问：What does the sentence mainly tell us? A. Getting up early is good for us. B. We should sleep more. C. Breakfast is important. D. School starts early.
    答：答案 A。句子说明早起能让你有更多时间准备一天，即早起对我们有好处。
  3. 3. （中考真题）阅读：My mother is a doctor. She works hard and often comes back home late. Though she is busy, she always makes time to help me with my lessons. 问：What is the best title for this passage? A. My Mother's Job B. My Busy but Kind Mother C. A Doctor's Day D. How to Be a Doctor
    答：答案 B。文章既写妈妈工作忙碌又写她抽空帮我学习，标题应兼顾"忙碌"与"关爱"，B 最贴切。
  4. 4. （中考真题）阅读：The book is very popular among teenagers because it tells a moving story about friendship. 问：The word "moving" in the passage means ______. A. 感人的 B. 移动的 C. 便宜的 D. 无聊的
    答：答案 A。由"关于友谊的故事受青少年欢迎"推断故事"感人"，moving 意为感人的。

💬 课后反馈

**常见错误**：细节题不看题干关键词凭印象选；主旨题被细节选项干扰；推断题凭常识过度推理；词义题孤立猜词。 **教学改进建议**：训练"题干—原文"定位划线法；用表格对比四大题型；每周一次限时训练模拟考试节奏。 **分层辅导**：学困生先过关细节题与应用文查找；学优生挑战推理判断与主旨归纳综合题。 **给家长的建议**：每天读 1-2 篇短文做 2-3 道题；摘抄好句积累词汇。
