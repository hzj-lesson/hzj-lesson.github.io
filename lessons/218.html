初中九年级英语《书面表达（中考写作）》教案

初中九年级英语书面表达（中考写作）

📝 备课方案

**教学目标** 1. 了解中考书面表达的常见题型（书信、电子邮件、记叙文、说明文、议论文）与评分标准（内容、语言、结构、书写）。 2. 掌握书信与电子邮件、记叙文的写作模板，做到结构清晰、要点齐全。 3. 积累并使用好词好句（高级词汇、宾语从句、定语从句、状语从句、过渡词），提升语言表达水平。 4. 掌握审题、列提纲、起草、修改、誊抄五步写作流程，能按要求完成 80-100 词的短文。 **教学重点**：书信模板与记叙文模板；写作五步流程的落实。 **教学难点**：要点的完整覆盖与过渡衔接；高级句式的自然运用而非生搬硬套；时态与语法的准确。 **教学方法**：范文教学法、过程写作法、仿写训练法、同伴互评法。 **教学准备**：多媒体课件、优秀范文集、常用过渡词与高级句型清单、作文评分量表。 **课时安排**：共 2 课时。第 1 课时讲题型与模板，示范书信写作全流程；第 2 课时练习记叙文与话题作文，开展互评与修改。

📖 课堂讲义 · 核心知识点

**一、题型与评分标准** 常见题型：书信、电子邮件、通知、记叙文、说明文、议论文。评分四方面：内容完整（要点齐全）、语言准确（无语法错误）、结构清晰（分段合理、过渡自然）、书写规范（字迹工整）。 **二、书信/电子邮件模板** 开头称呼：Dear ..., 首段（说明目的）：How is everything going? I'm writing to tell you about... 中段（展开内容）：First,... Second,... Besides,... What's more,... 尾段（总结期待）：I'm looking forward to your reply. 落款：Yours, Li Hua **三、记叙文模板** 开头点题：Last weekend, I had a wonderful experience. 经过：At first,... Then,... After that,... Finally,... 结尾感悟：From this experience, I have learned that... **四、好词好句** 短语：be good at, take part in, look forward to, make progress, be proud of, in order to。 句式：宾语从句 I believe that...；定语从句 The book which I like best...；状语从句 When I was ten,...；感叹句 What a wonderful day!

🔍 课堂讲义 · 重点难点解析

**重点一：审题与要点覆盖** 例题：以 My Favorite Subject 为题写作，要点：科目名称、喜欢原因、学习收获。 分析：按三个要点分三段写，先列提纲再动笔。 解答示例：My favorite subject is English. I like it because it is interesting and useful. Besides, my English teacher is very kind. By learning English, I have learned a lot. 小结：把题目要点一一列出，写完全文核对，确保不遗漏。 **难点一：过渡与衔接** 例题：如何让英语学习经历的叙述衔接自然？ 分析：用过渡词串联——表顺序 First/Then/Finally，表递进 Besides/What's more，表转折 However。 解答：My English was poor at first. However, I didn't give up. First, I listened to English songs. Besides, I practiced speaking. Therefore, my English improved a lot. 小结：过渡词让文章立刻连贯。 **难点二：避免中式英语** 例题：学生常写 I very like it，如何纠正？ 分析：very 不能直接修饰动词。 解答：应改为 I like it very much. 或 I really like it. 小结：常见中式英语还有 Study English is useful，应改为 Learning English is useful。

✏️ 课堂讲义 · 典型例题精讲

**例题 1（书信写作）** 题目：假设你是李华，给英国笔友 Tom 回信，介绍上个周末的活动，词数 80 左右。 范文：Dear Tom, I'm glad to hear from you. Last Saturday, I visited the science museum and watched a wonderful robot show. On Sunday, I stayed at home and read an interesting book. I think it was a relaxing and meaningful weekend. What about yours? I'm looking forward to your reply. Yours, Li Hua 点评：格式完整，分段清晰，用 I think 宾语从句，语言流畅。 **例题 2（记叙文写作）** 题目：以 A Meaningful Thing I Did 为题写一件事，含经过与收获，词数 80 左右。 范文：Last summer, I joined a volunteer group and helped clean up the park. At first, I found it tiring. However, when I saw the park become clean, I felt proud. I have learned that helping others brings us happiness. This experience is really meaningful to me. 点评：开头点题，用 At first/However 呈现变化，结尾升华主题，时态准确。

📋 分层作业（基础 / 中等 / 难 / 综合）

基础 · 基础巩固

  1. 1. 翻译句子：我非常喜欢英语，因为它很有趣。
    答：答案 I like English very much because it is interesting.。注意 very much 修饰动词 like，不能说 I very like。
  2. 2. 翻译句子：我每天早上读英语来提高我的口语。
    答：答案 I read English every morning to improve my spoken English.。to improve 作目的状语，improve 意为提高。
  3. 3. 翻译句子：我期待收到你的来信。
    答：答案 I'm looking forward to hearing from you.。look forward to 中 to 是介词，后接动名词 hearing；hear from 意为收到某人的信。
  4. 4. 连词成句：improved, I, my, listening, by, English, songs, to (用所给词写一句话)。
    答：答案 I improved my English by listening to songs.。by doing 表示"通过做某事"，listening 用动名词。

中等 · 能力提升

  1. 1. 翻译句子：这本书是我读过的书中最有趣的一本。
    答：答案 This book is the most interesting one that I have ever read.。最高级 + 定语从句 that I have ever read。
  2. 2. 翻译句子：当我遇到困难时，我的老师总是帮助我。
    答：答案 When I meet difficulties, my teacher always helps me.。when 引导时间状语从句，主句用一般现在时。
  3. 3. 翻译句子：做运动对我们的健康有好处。
    答：答案 Doing sports is good for our health.。动名词 doing sports 作主语，谓语用第三人称单数 is。
  4. 4. 句型转换：I think English is interesting. 改为含宾语从句的复合句：I think ______ ______ ______. 再写一句含定语从句的句子。
    答：答案 I think that English is interesting.（宾语从句）；英语定语从句示例：This is the book that I like best.。

难 · 综合挑战

  1. 1. 段落写作：用 3-5 句话介绍你最好的朋友（姓名、外貌性格、你们常做的事）。
    答：参考答案：My best friend is Wang Lin. He is tall and outgoing. He always helps me with my study. We often play basketball together after school. I am lucky to have such a good friend.。注意使用 and/always/often 使语言连贯。
  2. 2. 段落写作：写一段话介绍你的英语学习方法（至少三种方法）。
    答：参考答案：I have some good ways to learn English. First, I listen to English songs to improve my listening. Second, I keep a diary in English every day. Besides, I often watch English movies and talk with my classmates in English. These ways help me make great progress.。用 First/Second/Besides 使层次清晰。
  3. 3. 补全短文：My favorite season is spring. ______, it is warm and everything turns green. ______, I can fly kites with my friends. I like spring best ______ it makes me feel happy. 用合适的连接词填空。
    答：答案 First / Second / because。依次填 First（首先）、Second（其次）、because（因为），体现层次与因果。
  4. 4. 修改病句：Learning English are important for us. 找出并改正错误。
    答：答案把 are 改为 is。动名词短语 learning English 作主语，谓语用第三人称单数 is。

综合 · 中考真题对接

  1. 1. （中考真题）书面表达：假设你是李华，你的外国朋友 Mike 想了解中国的传统节日。请你给他写一封信，介绍一个你最喜欢的传统节日。要求：1. 节日名称与时间；2. 人们通常做什么；3. 你的感受。词数 80 左右。
    答：参考范文：Dear Mike, I'm glad to introduce my favorite traditional festival to you. It is the Mid-Autumn Festival, which usually comes in September or October. On that day, families get together and enjoy a big dinner. After dinner, we eat mooncakes and watch the bright moon in the yard. I like this festival because it means family reunion and makes me feel warm. I hope you can come to China to enjoy it one day. Yours, Li Hua。格式完整，要点齐全，用 which 引导定语从句、because 状语从句，结构清晰。
  2. 2. （中考真题）书面表达：请以 My Dream 为题写一篇短文。要求：1. 你的梦想是什么；2. 你为什么有这个梦想；3. 你打算怎样实现它。词数 80 左右。
    答：参考范文：Everyone has a dream. My dream is to be a doctor. I want to be a doctor because I want to help sick people and bring health to them. To make my dream come true, I will study hard at all subjects, especially biology and English. Besides, I will read more medical books and take part in some volunteer activities. I believe if I keep trying, my dream will come true.。结构为"梦想—原因—计划"，末句用 I believe + 宾语从句和 if 条件句提升表达。
  3. 3. （中考真题）书面表达：你校英语社团将举办"我的校园生活"征文活动，请你写一篇短文参加。要求：1. 校园生活的某一方面（学习、活动、友谊等）；2. 你的感受。词数 80 左右。
    答：参考范文：My school life is colorful and meaningful. I have many good teachers and classmates. In class, we learn knowledge and discuss questions together. After class, I often take part in different activities, such as the English club and the sports meeting. They make my school life full of fun. I have made some good friends here, and we help each other in study. I really enjoy my school life and I will remember it forever.。围绕"学习与活动"展开，结尾表达感受，用 such as、help each other 等好词。
  4. 4. （中考真题）书面表达：假如你是李华，请你给学校英语报投稿，谈谈"怎样保护环境"。要求：1. 至少提出两条建议；2. 词数 80 左右。
    答：参考范文：Protecting the environment is everyone's duty. There are many things we can do in our daily life. First, we should turn off the lights when we leave the room to save energy. Second, we can ride bikes or take buses instead of asking parents to drive us. Besides, we should sort the rubbish and plant more trees. If everyone does a little, the world will be much better. Let's take action together.。建议用 First/Second/Besides 分层，末句号召行动，用 If 条件句收尾。

💬 课后反馈

**常见错误**：要点遗漏不列提纲；中式英语（very like、Study English is useful）；无过渡词平铺直叙；时态混乱。 **教学改进建议**：落实审题—列提纲—起草—修改—誊抄五步流程；每周限时写作加互评批改；建立好词好句积累本定期仿写。 **分层辅导**：学困生保格式正确、要点齐全、简单句准确；学优生用复合句与高级词汇追求亮点。 **给家长的建议**：每周写一篇英文小短文；整理范文集考前背诵经典句型与模板。
