初中九年级英语九年级话题：学习方法与建议

📝 备课方案

**教学目标** 1. 掌握话题核心词汇与短语：take notes, review, memorize, practice, make a plan, do more reading 等。 2. 掌握表达建议的句型：You'd better..., Why not...?, It's a good idea to..., How about doing...?，并能正确运用。 3. 能就 How to learn English well 等话题进行口头与书面表达，写出结构清晰、建议具体的短文。 4. 通过听说读写综合活动，提升用英语解决学习实际问题的能力，培养良好学习习惯。 **教学重点**：话题词汇与句型的学习运用；建议类作文的写作框架。 **教学难点**：建议的具体化、条理化表达；提建议与征求意见句型的区分。 **教学方法**：话题教学法、情境交际法、任务型教学法、写作支架法。 **教学准备**：多媒体课件、话题词汇卡片、建议句型表、范文与写作学案。 **课时安排**：共 2 课时。第 1 课时学习话题词汇与句型，开展口语交际；第 2 课时指导话题写作并展示点评。

📖 课堂讲义 · 核心知识点

**一、话题核心词汇** 学习行为：take notes, review, memorize, practice, read aloud, keep a diary, look up, ask...for help。 学习方法：make a study plan, work in groups, pay attention to, learn...by heart。 学习品质：patient（耐心的）, active（积极的）, hard-working（勤奋的）。 **二、表达建议的句型** 1. You'd better (not) do sth. 2. Why not do sth.? / Why don't you do sth.? 3. How/What about doing sth.? 4. It's a good idea to do sth. 5. I think you should/shouldn't do sth. 6. If you want to..., you can... **三、话题作文框架（How to learn English well）** 首段点题：English is important. Here is my advice on how to learn it well. 中段分条：First,... Second,... Besides,...（每条用建议句型 + 具体做法） 尾段总结：In a word, if you keep practicing, your English will improve.

🔍 课堂讲义 · 重点难点解析

**重点一：建议句型的正确使用** 例题：用两种方式译"你最好每天听英语"。 解答：You'd better listen to English every day. 或 Why not listen to English every day? 小结：You'd better + 动词原形；Why not + 动词原形，后接动词原形最常考。 **难点一：把建议写具体** 例题：只说"多读书"太空泛，如何具体化？ 解答：You'd better read English stories or newspapers for half an hour every day. 小结：好建议 = 建议句型 + 具体做法（做什么、怎么做、何时做）。 **难点二：提建议与征求意见** 例题：向别人征求意见与给建议的表达有何不同？ 解答：征求意见用 Could you give me some advice on...?；提建议用 You should... / Why not...?。 小结："我"提问用 Could you...?，"我"给建议用 You'd better...。

✏️ 课堂讲义 · 典型例题精讲

**例题 1（单选：话题句型）** 题目：— My English is poor. — ______ reading English books every day? A. Why not B. Why don't C. What about D. Would you like 解析：空后是动名词 reading，What about + doing 正确；Why not/Why don't 接原形，Would you like 接 to do。 答案：C。 方法小结：看空后的动词形式选句型——doing 配 What/How about，原形配 Why not/You'd better。 **例题 2（话题写作）** 题目：以 How to learn English well 为题写一篇短文，至少提出三条建议。 范文：English is becoming more and more important. First, you'd better read English aloud every morning to practice your pronunciation. Second, why not keep a diary in English? It helps improve your writing. Besides, it's a good idea to watch English movies. In a word, learning English needs practice and patience. I hope these suggestions are useful to you. 点评：首段点题，中段用 First/Second/Besides 引出三条建议且句型多样，尾段总结并表达祝愿。

📋 分层作业（基础 / 中等 / 难 / 综合）

基础 · 基础巩固

  1. 1. 话题词汇单选：The best way to learn new words is to make ______ and review them often. A. notes B. plans C. friends D. mistakes
    答：答案 A。make notes 记笔记，与复习新单词呼应；make plans 制定计划、make friends 交朋友、make mistakes 犯错。
  2. 2. 话题词汇单选：If you want to improve your listening, you can ______ to English songs. A. listen B. listening C. listened D. listens
    答：答案 A。can 后接动词原形，listen to 听。
  3. 3. 话题句型：你最好早睡早起，这样上课才有精神。译成英文。
    答：答案 You'd better go to bed early and get up early so that you can stay active in class.。You'd better + 动词原形。
  4. 4. 话题句型：为什么不多阅读一些英文故事呢？译成英文。
    答：答案 Why not read more English stories?。Why not + 动词原形，表示建议。

中等 · 能力提升

  1. 1. 句型转换：Why don't you ask your teacher for help? 改为同义句：Why ______ ask your teacher for help?
    答：答案 not。Why don't you do sth? 与 Why not do sth? 同义，都表建议。
  2. 2. 句型运用：用 It's a good idea to... 造一个关于学习英语的建议句。
    答：参考答案：It's a good idea to practice English with your classmates.。注意 to 后接动词原形。
  3. 3. 补全对话：A: I can't remember the grammar rules. B: ______ writing them in a notebook?（给出建议）
    答：答案 What about / How about。How/What about + doing 表示"……怎么样"，提出建议。
  4. 4. 翻译：认真听讲和及时复习是学好功课的关键。
    答：答案 Listening carefully in class and reviewing in time are the keys to learning lessons well.。动名词短语作主语；the key to doing sth 中 to 是介词。

难 · 综合挑战

  1. 1. 语篇填空：用方框中的词填空（do, listen, improve, make, ask）：To learn English well, we should ___1___ more reading. We can ___2___ to English programs to ___3___ our listening. It's also helpful to ___4___ a study plan and ___5___ our teachers for advice. 1-2 空。
    答：答案 1. do（do more reading 多阅读）；2. listen（listen to 听）。
  2. 2. 语篇填空：（接上题）3-5 空。
    答：答案 3. improve（improve our listening 提高听力）；4. make（make a plan 制定计划）；5. ask（ask...for advice 向……征求意见）。
  3. 3. 段落写作：写 4-5 句话，给你的同学提供学好英语的建议（至少两条，用上两种建议句型）。
    答：参考答案：Learning English well needs good methods. First, you'd better read English aloud for twenty minutes every morning. It can help you with your pronunciation. Second, why not watch some English films in your free time? You can learn a lot of useful expressions from them. I believe if you keep trying, you will make great progress.。用了 you'd better 与 why not 两种句型，并用 I believe 宾语从句结尾。
  4. 4. 书面表达（话题作文）：请以 How to improve my study 为题写一篇短文。要求：1. 你的学习方法；2. 遇到困难时的做法；3. 词数 80 左右。
    答：参考范文：Study is very important for us students. Here is how I improve my study. In class, I always listen to the teachers carefully and take notes. After class, I review what I have learned and finish my homework on time. When I meet difficulties, I never give up. I often ask my teachers or classmates for help. Besides, I make a study plan and follow it every day. With these good habits, my study has improved a lot. I will keep going and make more progress.。首段点题，中段分"课内/课后/遇困难"展开，尾段总结，用 When 状语从句、with 介词短语提升表达。

综合 · 中考真题对接

  1. 1. （中考真题）— I have trouble in learning grammar. — ______ asking your English teacher for help? A. Why not B. What about C. Why don't D. Would you mind
    答：答案 B。空后是动名词 asking，What about doing 提出建议；Why not/Why don't 后接动词原形。
  2. 2. （中考真题）The more you practice speaking English, the ______ you will speak it. A. good B. well C. better D. best
    答：答案 C。the more..., the better... 越……越……，speak it better 更流利。
  3. 3. （中考真题）It's a good habit ______ English every morning. A. read B. to read C. reading D. reads
    答：答案 B。It's + 名词 + to do sth 是固定句型，it 作形式主语，真正主语是不定式 to read。
  4. 4. （中考真题）书面表达：你校英语角将举行"分享学习方法"活动，请写一篇英语短文，介绍你的一种高效学习方法并说明理由。要求：词数 80 左右。
    答：参考范文：Hello, everyone. Today I'd like to share a good way to learn English with you. That is keeping a diary in English. I started to do it last year. Every day, I write down what I have seen, heard and thought in English. At first it was difficult, but now it becomes easier and easier. Keeping a diary helps me remember more words and sentences. It also improves my writing and thinking. I think it is really a useful way. Why not have a try?。以"方法—做法—理由—号召"结构展开，用 that is 解释、动名词作主语，结尾 Why not 呼应话题。

💬 课后反馈

**常见错误**：建议句型后接形式错误（Why not + doing、You'd better + to do）；建议笼统缺乏具体做法；征求意见与提建议句型混淆。 **教学改进建议**：句型操练卡强化接续规则；口语活动要求每条建议说具体；归纳作文框架并仿写。 **分层辅导**：学困生背熟建议句型与话题词汇；学优生要求建议多样化、理由充分并用复合句。 **给家长的建议**：把建议句型用在家庭交流中；帮助建立话题词汇本。
