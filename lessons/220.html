初中九年级英语《九年级话题：成长与改变》教案

初中九年级英语九年级话题：成长与改变

📝 备课方案

**教学目标** 1. 掌握话题核心词汇与短语：grow up, independent, confident, brave, used to do, get used to doing, be proud of 等。 2. 掌握表达变化与成长的句型：I used to be... but now I...; Thanks to..., I...; I have learned that...。 3. 能围绕"我的变化"进行口头与书面表达，写出成长故事与感悟。 4. 通过回顾自身成长经历，树立积极人生态度，学会感恩与自我激励。 **教学重点**：used to do 与 be/get used to doing 的辨析；成长类作文写作框架。 **教学难点**：used to 与 be used to doing 的区别；把成长故事写得具体、有情感。 **教学方法**：情境教学法、任务型教学法、支架式写作法、同伴交流法。 **教学准备**：话题词汇卡、句型对比表、范文学案。 **课时安排**：共 2 课时。第 1 课时学习话题词汇与句型，讲述自身变化；第 2 课时指导话题写作。

📖 课堂讲义 · 核心知识点

**一、话题核心词汇** 成长与品质：grow up, develop, independent, confident, brave, patient, responsible。 变化表达：change, improve, used to do（过去常常）, get used to doing（习惯于）, be different from。 情感态度：be proud of, be thankful to, make progress, overcome difficulties。 **二、表达变化与成长的句型** 1. I used to be shy, but now I am confident. 2. I used to be afraid of..., but now I... 3. Thanks to my teachers' help, I have made great progress. 4. I have learned that nothing is impossible if I work hard. 5. Not only did I improve my grades, but I also became more outgoing. **三、话题作文框架（My Changes）** 首段点题：I have changed a lot in the past three years. 中段写变化：In the past, I used to... But now I...（从性格、学习、爱好等方面写） 尾段感悟：These changes make me a better person. I'm thankful to...

🔍 课堂讲义 · 重点难点解析

**重点一：used to 与 be used to doing 的辨析** 例题：My grandfather ______ taking a walk after supper, but now he doesn't. A. used to B. is used to C. was used to D. got used to 分析：空后是动名词 taking，排除 used to（后接原形）；"过去习惯、现在不了"用一般过去时，故选 was used to。 解答：选 C。 小结：used to do（过去常常）；be/get used to doing（习惯于）；be used to do（被用来做，被动）。 **难点一：把成长故事写具体** 例题：只说"我以前胆小，现在勇敢"太空泛，如何具体化？ 解答：Two years ago, I was afraid to speak in class. But my teacher encouraged me to join the English corner. After I spoke there, I felt less nervous. Now I can give a speech in front of the whole school. 小结：成长故事 = 过去不足 + 转变契机 + 现在改变 + 感悟。 **难点二：not only...but also... 的倒装** 例题：把"我不仅学会了英语，还交了很多朋友"译成英文。 解答：Not only did I learn English well, but I also made many friends. 小结：not only 置于句首时，其所在分句用部分倒装（助动词 + 主语 + 谓语）。

✏️ 课堂讲义 · 典型例题精讲

**例题 1（used to 系列）** 题目：He ______ in the city for two years, so he is used to ______ there. A. has lived; living B. lived; live C. has lived; live D. lives; living 解析：for two years 用现在完成时 has lived；be used to doing 表示"习惯于"，用 living。 答案：A。 方法小结：for/since + 时间段用现在完成时；be/get used to 后接动名词。 **例题 2（话题写作）** 题目：以 My Changes 为题写一篇短文，介绍你初中三年来的变化。 范文：In the past, I used to be lazy and always put off my homework. I also used to be shy. However, everything changed after I joined the school football team. I learned to work with others and became active. Not only have I improved my study, but I have also made many friends. I'm thankful to my teachers for helping me become a better person. 点评：首段点题，中段"过去—转变—现在"，用 however 衔接，尾段用 not only...but also... 总结并表达感恩。

📋 分层作业（基础 / 中等 / 难 / 综合）

基础 · 基础巩固

  1. 1. 话题词汇单选：Don't be ______. Believe in yourself and try again. A. brave B. confident C. shy D. active
    答：答案 C。后句"相信自己，再试一次"暗示不要"害羞"，shy。
  2. 2. 话题词汇单选：My sister ______ dancing at the age of five, and now she is good at it. A. is interested in B. was interested in C. has interested D. interested
    答：答案 B。at the age of five 是过去时间，用一般过去时 was interested in。
  3. 3. 话题句型：我过去很胖，但现在我很健康。译成英文。
    答：答案 I used to be heavy, but now I am healthy.。used to do 表示过去常常。
  4. 4. 话题句型：我已习惯了早起。译成英文。
    答：答案 I am/get used to getting up early.。be/get used to doing 表示"习惯于做"，注意用动名词 getting。

中等 · 能力提升

  1. 1. 辨析选择：The knife is used ______ bread. / I used ______ early when I was a student. / I am used ______ early now. 用 to cut / to get up / to getting up 填空。
    答：答案 to cut / to get up / to getting up。be used to do 被用来做；used to do 过去常常；be used to doing 习惯于。
  2. 2. 话题句型：多亏你的帮助，我通过了考试。译成英文（用 Thanks to）。
    答：答案 Thanks to your help, I passed the exam.。Thanks to 后接名词短语 your help。
  3. 3. 话题句型：我不仅提高了成绩，还变得更加自信。（用 Not only...but also...）
    答：答案 Not only did I improve my grades, but I also became more confident.。not only 置于句首时用部分倒装 did I improve。
  4. 4. 补全句子：我过去害怕在公共场合演讲，但现在我能从容地发表演讲。I used to be afraid of ______, but now I can give a speech ______.
    答：答案 speaking in public / with confidence。be afraid of + 动名词；give a speech with confidence 自信地演讲。

难 · 综合挑战

  1. 1. 语篇填空：用方框中的词填空（become, afraid, thanks, changed, brave）：Three years ago, I was ___1___ of speaking English. My life ___2___ after I joined the English club. ___3___ to my teacher's help, I ___4___ more and more confident. Now I am ___5___ enough to talk with foreigners. 1-2 空。
    答：答案 1. afraid（be afraid of 害怕）；2. changed（我的生活在加入英语俱乐部后改变了）。
  2. 2. 语篇填空：（接上题）3-5 空。
    答：答案 3. Thanks（Thanks to 多亏）；4. became（become more and more confident 变得越来越自信）；5. brave（brave enough 足够勇敢）。
  3. 3. 段落写作：用 4-5 句话写一写你初中阶段性格或习惯上的一个改变（用上 used to）。
    答：参考答案：When I entered middle school, I used to be quiet and seldom answered questions in class. I was afraid of making mistakes. Later, my deskmate encouraged me to speak up. Little by little, I got used to sharing my ideas with others. Now I have become an active student and my grades have improved, too.。体现"过去—转变—现在"，用 used to、got used to 与 become。
  4. 4. 书面表达（话题作文）：请以 Growing Up 为题写一篇短文。要求：1. 成长过程中你最大的变化；2. 变化的原因；3. 词数 80 左右。
    答：参考范文：Growing up is a journey full of changes. The biggest change in me is that I have become independent. When I was a child, I always depended on my parents for everything. I couldn't even do my own homework without their help. However, after I went to a boarding school, I had to look after myself. I learned to wash clothes, make beds and plan my time. Now I can deal with many problems by myself. I have learned that growing up means taking responsibility. I'm proud of my changes and I will keep growing.。结构为"最大变化—原因—感悟"，用 however 转折、定语从句与 I have learned 句型，结尾升华主题。

综合 · 中考真题对接

  1. 1. （中考真题）My little sister ______ up late, but now she goes to bed early. A. used to stay B. is used to staying C. used to staying D. was used to stay
    答：答案 A。but now she goes to bed early 表明"过去常常熬夜"、现在不了，用 used to stay。
  2. 2. （中考真题）He used to ______ problems with his English, but now he does well in it. A. have B. having C. had D. has
    答：答案 A。used to + 动词原形，have problems with 在……方面有问题。
  3. 3. （中考真题）— What do you think of your English teacher? — She is very patient. ______ her help, I have made great progress. A. Thanks to B. Instead of C. As for D. Because
    答：答案 A。Thanks to 多亏、由于，后接名词 her help；because 后接句子。
  4. 4. （中考真题）书面表达：临近毕业，回顾初中生活，你一定成长了不少。请以 I Have Grown Up 为题写一篇短文，讲述你的一次成长经历及感悟。要求：词数 80 左右。
    答：参考范文：I have grown up a lot during the three years in middle school. I still remember my first speech competition. At that time, I used to be too nervous to speak in public. When it was my turn, my heart beat fast and I nearly gave up. But my best friend smiled at me and said, "You can do it!" Taking a deep breath, I walked onto the stage and finished my speech. To my surprise, I got the first prize. From then on, I have become braver and more confident. I have learned that courage comes from trying. This experience will always encourage me to face challenges bravely.。以一件具体事写成长，运用 used to、时间顺序词、对话与感悟，情感真实、语言丰富。

💬 课后反馈

**常见错误**：used to 三种结构混淆；成长故事只有结论没有细节；not only 置于句首时忘记倒装；前后时态不一致。 **教学改进建议**：用三栏对照表强化三种 used to 结构；用时间线写作法先列时间点再成文；范文精读仿写。 **分层辅导**：学困生掌握 used to 基本用法，写出"过去 vs 现在"对比句；学优生要求事例具体、用倒装等高分句式。 **给家长的建议**：和孩子一起回忆成长故事积累素材；鼓励用英语向家人介绍自己的变化。
